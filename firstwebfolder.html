Piano in my Life

Teachers

I started playing piano at the age of five and my first teacher was my aunt. Then when my family moved to a new house, we had to switch piano teachers. The new teacher I got only allowed me to play classical music. I think she was a good teacher for the basics. However, learning with her was so boring. This boredom drove me insane so we changed to this other teacher who was honestly terrifying. She was an old granny who did not care about my opinions. I then quit piano for two years during COVID. Since then I've been playing piano with my aunt again.

Performances

I've been performing solo piano ever since I've started. One recital in spring and one in winter. Every year I've played piano I've always been working toward playing at recitals. I've also played a couple times for my church. I also remember playing in a few competitions but I hadn't really had my heart in piano until about 2 years ago. Then I started playing like it mattered to me. It changed everything. Last year I performed at 2 competitions seriously and won first in both! Currently I'm striving to complete my piano piece for a competition in March.
